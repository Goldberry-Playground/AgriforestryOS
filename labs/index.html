At The Grove Nursery · Friday 2026-10-30

Help us train the planner

Two prototypes, nothing to install. Open one on your phone, build something for land you actually know, and change whatever we got wrong. Everything you do stays on your phone until you copy your session into the feedback form at the end.

Station 1 · everyone

The planner

lab.atthegrovenursery.com/planner
  1. Type your ZIP. Pick what you are building and what the ground is like.
  2. Remove what you would not plant. Add from the suggested list.
  3. Turn on tester mode, dispute a fact, suggest a plant we should carry.
Then: Copy my session, and paste it into the feedback form.
Station 2 · consultants and farmers with acreage

The consult flow

lab.atthegrovenursery.com/consult
  1. New client. Enter a property you know: areas, acres, a practice for each.
  2. Pick the goals, draft the plan, click a gap or a pick to see why.
  3. Edit a section the way you would write it. Approve the plan.
Then: Copy my session, and paste it into the feedback form.
Station 3 · before you leave

Feedback form

lab.atthegrovenursery.com/feedback
  1. Paste your session.
  2. What would you have planted instead, and why?
  3. Would you use this to plan your own ground, or to plan for a client?
Takes about three minutes. Your name is optional.

Hosted for the day on our own domain. Nothing here touches the shop or your account. Prices shown in the consult flow are placeholders.
